The True Cost of Car Ownership

Every car has a sticker. And a shadow.

Car ads show you a monthly payment and stop there. The math the dealership leaves off — the interest, the depreciation, the retirement that didn't happen — is just compound interest pointed at you. Three quick beats, then the calculator.

What the salesperson says
$787/mo

We can get you in for under eight hundred a month.

What that $787 is actually buying the bank
  • Loan principal $45,326
  • Interest rate (APR) 6.4%
  • Term 69 months
  • Total of payments $54,303
  • Required full-coverage insurance + $220/mo
Of which is interest $8,977

The car costs the same. The math just costs more.

$787 above is the average. Almost one in five new car loans now run over a thousand dollars a month — that's not the high end, that's the new high end.NPR · Oct 2025

Most people only ever check the monthly. $787 sounds doable. $8,977 in interest plus $1,000+ a year in mandatory insurance — that's the half the brochure leaves off.

Compound interest works for you when your investment outpaces your debt. It works against you when your debt outpaces your investment — or when what you bought with the debt is losing value. That's the only sentence in this whole piece you actually have to remember.
